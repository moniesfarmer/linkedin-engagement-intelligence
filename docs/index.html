06Key Findings & Recommendations
📊
Resource posts dominateActionable resource posts average the highest impressions — consistently outperform motivational content.
🎯
CTA posts drive commentsPosts ending with a specific, experience-based question get significantly more comments.
📅
Mid-week posts winTuesday–Thursday posts consistently outperform weekend posts. Publish Mon–Thu for maximum reach.
✍️
Sweet spot: 180–260 wordsPosts in this range show the highest engagement rates. Under 100 words consistently underperform.
🚫
Avoid motivational contentMindset/motivational posts average the lowest impressions of any content category.
🔥
AI + Career topics leadPosts combining AI insight with career relevance achieve the highest engagement rates.
