06 Key Findings & Recommendations
📊
Resource posts dominate Job listings & curated resource posts average 303 impressions — 3× higher than motivational content. Prioritize actionable content.
🎯
CTA posts drive comments Posts ending with a specific, experience-based question get 2× more comments than posts with vague or no CTA.
📅
Wednesday is peak day Wednesday posts average 268 impressions vs 95 for weekend posts. Prioritize Mon–Thu for publishing.
✍️
Sweet spot: 180–260 words Posts in the 180–260 word range show the highest engagement rates. Under 100 words consistently underperform.
🚫
Avoid motivational content "Growth mindset" and "Good morning" posts average only 75 impressions — the lowest of any content category.
🔥
Career + AI topics lead Career-topic posts achieve 1.79% avg engagement rate — above LinkedIn's 1.0% "good" benchmark.
